# SkyMessage design language — "Twilight sky"

The final look of SkyMessage. Every screen, dialog and control follows this document; `docs/MOTION.md` covers motion
and gestures, `docs/UI-KIT.md` the components' API. Tokens and kit styles live in
`src/Client/SkyMessage.Client.UI/wwwroot/css/app.css` — read its token blocks before styling anything.

## 1. Idea

SkyMessage lives in the sky. Every screen is a piece of it — a lavender-to-peach **dawn** in the light theme, a deep
indigo **night** with an aurora, stars and the odd shooting star in the dark theme. Interface surfaces are **frosted
glass** floating over that sky, lit by violet gradients and warm sunlight. Shapes are soft: **squircles** and
**capsules**; anything that can float (headers, the dock, the composer, toasts) floats, detached from the screen
edges. The app is for a family across two countries, so it must feel warm, calm and alive — and stay readable for
older relatives on budget Android phones.

### It must never look like Telegram

| Telegram has | SkyMessage has instead |
|---|---|
| Blue/cyan accent | Violet → orchid gradient (`--sm-grad`) with warm peach/gold light (`--sm-warm`, `--sm-gold`) |
| Circle avatars | Squircle avatars (`.sm-avatar` is masked with `--sm-squircle`) — never `border-radius: 50%` on avatars |
| Bubble tails | No tails. Large radius (`--sm-bubble-radius`, default 20px); consecutive messages tighten the sender-side corners to 6px |
| Doodle-pattern wallpapers | The living sky (`<SkyBackdrop>`): gradient + drifting light orbs + stars at night |
| Pale green/blue outgoing bubbles | Outgoing bubbles in the accent gradient (`--sm-bubble-out-bg`) with white text and a soft gloss |
| Paper-plane send icon | Arrow-up in a glowing gradient orb |
| Pencil FAB, hamburger drawer | Floating glass dock with a sliding gradient pill; "new chat" is a gradient button in the header |
| Hairline separators in lists | Air between rows; grouped rows sit on frosted cards (`.sm-section__body`) |
| Saturated square settings icons | Pastel squircle tiles tinted by the row color (`.sm-list-item__icon`) |
| Plain white app bars | Transparent large-title headers over the sky that turn into a floating glass bar on scroll |

### No AI clichés ("нейрослоп")

The owner rejected the first logo as AI-generated-looking. SkyMessage must look crafted by people:
- **No four-point sparkles** (✦), "magic" stars, wands or sparkle particles — not as decoration, not in bursts, not
  in the logo. Night stars are tiny round dots.
- **No gradient-filled text**; titles and the wordmark are solid colors.
- **No neon glows or halos.** Colored shadows stay small and soft (`--sm-glow`), on one primary control at most.
- **No rainbow aurora blobs.** The sky's lights are soft and low-saturation, like a real dawn or night sky.
- Illustrations are simple, flat, with few colors and a clear idea (a small cloud, a moon, a horizon) — never glossy
  3D, glowing orbs or generic "AI" imagery. When in doubt, leave it out.

### Nothing standard-Android

No Roboto (fonts: **Rubik** for text, **Montserrat** for display — `--sm-font`, `--sm-font-display`), no Material
ripple, no Material FAB / tab indicator / switches / checkboxes, no native `<select>` (use `Kit/Select` → our choice
sheet), no `<input type="color">`, no native `<video controls>`/`<audio controls>`, no `alert/confirm/prompt`, no
default scrollbars, no tap highlight. Everything a user touches is ours.

## 2. Color

All colors come from tokens; never hard-code a hex in a component except inside illustrations. Key tokens (both
themes defined in `app.css`, accent/sky ones regenerated by `ThemeCatalog` for the user's accent and sky):

- **Accent**: `--sm-accent` (solid, for text/icons/links), `--sm-accent-2` (partner hue), `--sm-grad` (the signature
  135° gradient — primary buttons, badges, outgoing bubbles, active pills), `--sm-grad-soft` (pale tint for selected
  states and art backgrounds), `--sm-accent-soft` (flat tint), `--sm-on-accent` (text on the gradient), `--sm-glow`
  and `--sm-glow-s` (colored shadows under gradient elements).
- **Warm light**: `--sm-warm` (peach), `--sm-gold`, `--sm-grad-warm` — decoration, mentions, "favorite", the sun.
- **Status**: `--sm-danger` / `--sm-grad-danger` (rose), `--sm-success` / `--sm-grad-success` (mint), `--sm-warning`,
  `--sm-online` (mint, with a glow).
- **Neutrals**: `--sm-bg`, `--sm-surface`, `--sm-surface-2/3`, `--sm-text`, `--sm-text-2`, `--sm-text-3`,
  `--sm-border`, `--sm-divider` — all tinted violet, never grey.
- **Glass**: `--sm-glass` (floating chrome), `--sm-glass-strong` (dialogs, menus, sheets), `--sm-card` (cards and
  grouped lists), `--sm-glass-solid` / `--sm-card-solid` (lite fallbacks), `--sm-blur`, `--sm-edge` (1px light rim +
  top highlight; combine: `box-shadow: var(--sm-edge), var(--sm-shadow-2)`).
- **Sky**: `--sm-sky` (base gradient), `--sm-orb-1/2/3`, `--sm-stars` (0 light, 1 dark),
  `--sm-chat-wallpaper` / `--sm-chat-bg` (same sky, for chats).
- **Bubbles**: `--sm-bubble-out-bg` (gradient), `--sm-bubble-out` (solid midpoint, for borders/quote bars),
  `--sm-bubble-out-text`, `--sm-bubble-out-meta`, `--sm-bubble-in`, `--sm-bubble-in-text`, `--sm-bubble-in-meta`.
- **Elevation**: `--sm-shadow-1` (cards), `--sm-shadow-2` (floating chrome), `--sm-shadow-3` (dialogs, menus, toasts).

Contrast: body text ≥ 4.5:1 on its surface (the accent pairs are deep enough for white text). Gradient fills carry
white text only.

## 3. Type

- `--sm-font` **Rubik** (400, 700, italics): messages, previews, body text, inputs.
- `--sm-font-display` **Montserrat** (500, 700): titles, names, buttons, tab labels, badges, section overlines, numbers
  that matter (timers, counters).
- Scale: large title 30px/700 (`.sm-title-xl`), screen title 22px (`.sm-title-l`), dialog/section title 18–20px,
  names 15.5–16px Montserrat 600 (renders 700), body 16px Rubik, secondary 14px, meta 12–12.5px, overline 11px
  uppercase +0.09em (`.sm-overline`). Letter-spacing −0.01…−0.025em on display sizes.
- Weights: only 400/500 or 600/700 exist; never rely on 300 or 800.

## 4. Shape & depth

- Radii: `--sm-radius-xs 8`, `s 12`, `m 16`, `l 22`, `xl 28`, `2xl 34`; capsules `999px`.
- Avatars, icon tiles, empty-state art, swatches: **squircle** (`.sm-squircle` or `mask: var(--sm-squircle)`).
- Buttons, search, chips, segmented pills, toasts' actions: capsules. Cards, dialogs: 28px. Sheets: 34px top corners.
- Floating chrome sits 8–12px from screen edges (plus safe-area insets), with `--sm-shadow-2` and the glass edge.
- Depth comes from glass + colored glow, not grey drop shadows.

## 5. The sky and decoration

- `<SkyBackdrop />` (Kit): base gradient, three drifting soft lights, clouds (light), two twinkling star fields and a
  rare comet (dark). Params: `Variant` (App/Chat), `Fixed`, `Still`, `Comet`. Put it as the
  first child of a positioned container; content above it needs `position: relative` (or `z-index`).
- Where: behind root screens (chat list, contacts, calls, settings), every chat (Variant=Chat), auth screens (richest),
  the call screen (its own aurora), boot splash. Pages pushed on top (settings subpages, profile, group info) use
  `Still="true"` so only the visible sky moves. Covered layers (`[inert]`) pause automatically.
- Illustrations: draw our own simple flat SVG (clouds, moon, horizon, round stars) in the palette. Never
  Telegram/stock art, never sparkles.
- Logo: `<Logo />` (tile), `Mark` (the symbol alone), `Animated` (a gentle idle motion).

## 6. Components (kit, app.css)

- **Buttons**: primary = accent capsule with a soft shadow; secondary = white/glass capsule with rim; tonal = accent tint;
  danger = rose gradient. Press: spring scale 0.95.
- **Icon buttons**: 42px circles. `Glass` variant (`.sm-icon-btn--glass`) for headers and anything over the sky;
  `Filled` = gradient orb with glow; `OnMedia` over photos.
- **Fields**: 54px, radius 18, lavender fill; focus = a crisp accent rim (no halo); errors shake once.
- **Select**: `Kit/Select` is a field-like button opening the choice sheet. **Switch**: gradient capsule, pearl knob
  with spring. **Checkbox/radio**: squircle/circle, gradient fill, the check draws itself. **Slider**: gradient fill,
  pearl knob with halo.
- **Lists**: rows 56px+, no separators; grouped rows on frosted cards with 6px inner padding, rows round to 20px on
  press. Icon tiles are pastel squircles (`--icon-bg` sets the hue).
- **Badges**: accent pills; muted = surface-3; mention = warm gradient.
- **Dialogs**: glass-strong, 28px, spring in (`sm-dialog-in`). **Sheets**: 34px corners,
  spring overshoot. **Menus**: glass-strong cards unfolding (`sm-menu-in`), accent icons, 15px row radius.
- **Toasts**: dark glass capsules (both themes) with the icon in a gradient disc; spring in from below.
- **Tabs**: underline = accent thread; pill = gradient capsule. **Segmented**: lavender track, white pill.
- **Spinner**: a gradient comet arc. **Skeletons**: lavender with a shine. **Empty states**: floating glass squircle
  with the icon, display-font title.

## 7. Screens (phone first; wide layouts follow the same materials)

- **Root screens** (Chats, Contacts, Calls, Settings): sky behind; large title (30px Montserrat) and glass header
  actions over the sky; when the list scrolls, a compact glass bar fades in with the 17px title. Content starts
  below; the floating **dock** (glass capsule, 12px from the sides/bottom + inset) holds the four tabs with a sliding
  gradient pill behind the active icon (+ label), badges with glow. Lists get bottom padding for the dock.
- **Chat list rows**: squircle avatar 54px (unread: a thin accent ring), name in display font, preview in Rubik
  text-2, time top-right, unread gradient badge, pinned/muted glyphs in text-3. Rows on the sky inside one frosted
  panel or as airy rows with press highlight — no separators. Swipe actions keep working (MOTION.md).
- **Chat**: the chat sky (Variant=Chat). Header = floating glass capsule (8px from the top inset and sides) with back,
  squircle avatar, name/status, call actions. Bubbles: incoming = surface (white/indigo) with `--sm-shadow-1`;
  outgoing = accent gradient + gloss; group sender names in their avatar colors; time + status inside the bubble.
  Date chips and the unread divider are glass capsules. Composer = floating glass capsule (attach, text, emoji) with
  the send/mic **orb** (52px accent circle with a soft shadow) beside it; the scroll-down button is a glass circle above it.
- **Settings/profile**: hero card with a large squircle avatar inside a thin accent ring, name in display font;
  grouped glass cards with pastel tiles.
- **Auth** (welcome/login/register): the sky (soft lights, clouds or stars, comet at night), the animated logo, the
  wordmark in a solid color, forms on a glass card, staggered entrance.
- **Calls**: a calm deep night sky with soft low-saturation light, avatar squircle 132px with subtle pulsing rings
  (`sm-ring`), glass control circles 64px, rose gradient hang-up. Calls list rows like chat rows; missed = rose.
- **Media viewer**: night background, glass top/bottom bars.

## 8. Motion signature (details: MOTION.md)

Springs for things arriving (`--sm-spring`, `--sm-spring-soft`), decelerate for movement, accelerate for exits.
Page content may rise in once on first render (`.sm-stagger`). Ambient motion (sky, logo, rings) is slow
and subtle; it stops with reduced motion and in `data-fx="lite"`.

## 9. Performance rules

- Animate only `transform` and `opacity`. No animated `filter`, `backdrop-filter`, `box-shadow` on lists.
- `backdrop-filter` only on floating chrome, cards, dialogs, menus, toasts — never on list rows or bubbles.
- `html[data-fx="lite"]` (≤ 2 GB devices, set by boot.js; `localStorage "sm.fx"` overrides) removes blur (solid
  `--sm-*-solid` surfaces) and freezes the sky. Any new glass surface must add its lite fallback.
- Every ambient animation must be inside something that pauses under `[inert]` or be cheap enough to ignore.
